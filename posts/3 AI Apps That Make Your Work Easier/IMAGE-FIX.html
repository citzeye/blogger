Every useful AI app does the same thing underneath: it takes the tedious part of the work away from you. The research you would have skimmed. The draft you would have started badly. The fifteen small steps between two systems that somebody has to click through by hand.

None of that is glamorous, and that is exactly why it is where the real value is. The person using an AI tool well is not doing anything clever — they are finishing the boring part in three minutes instead of forty, and spending the rest of the time on the part that needs a human.

Three apps cover most of that ground for a working adult: one for writing and thinking, one for voice, and one for the clicking between tools that eats an hour a week. Here is what each one genuinely takes off your plate, what it costs, and what you would still have to do yourself.

If your day is full of drafting, summarizing, recording or copying data between apps, you will feel the difference inside a week. If your work is mostly physical or purely creative, these will not help much, and it is better to know that now.
